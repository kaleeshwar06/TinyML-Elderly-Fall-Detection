Design and Development of a Context-Aware TinyML Wearable for Elderly Fall Detection,
Sensor-Derived Severity Assessment, and Post-Fall BPM–SpO₂ Monitoring

A four-stage ML pipeline trained on a 116,988-window, 6-axis IMU dataset. The proposed TinyFallNet (Multi-Scale Depthwise SE-ResCNN) delivers clinically competitive fall sensitivity with only 20,975 parameters — deployable on embedded microcontrollers.
